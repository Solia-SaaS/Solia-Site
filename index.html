One system for the whole of your life — plan it, live it, measure it, improve it.

Schedule

Your day, planned before it starts

Wake to wake, your day is a chain of blocks — work, training, meals, rest — minted from your recurring structure and tuned each night for tomorrow. Speak to it, drag it, or let it plan itself.

  • Voice-first planning — just say what's changing
  • Recurring weekly structure with one-off adjustments
  • Plan vs. actual: see the day you meant next to the day you lived
