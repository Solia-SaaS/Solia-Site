01 — Why Solia exists

Help make more people's dreams come true

Everyone has dreams. Almost no one lives them.

Why people's dreams fail

  • Life is hard, becoming who you want to be is even harder
  • A plan that actually accomplishes your goals isn't easy to make
  • As life keeps changing, plans and actions drift apart
  • Progress goes unmeasured, so motivation fades
  • No structure, no check-ins, no accountability
  • You have to work at it every single day
  • And the tools meant to help? They fail you too

How today's tools fail

  • Slow, unnatural input and complicated interfaces
  • You build every plan yourself, without guidance
  • No insight into whether your plan is good or working
  • What you planned vs what you did in reality isn't captured
  • Rigid templates that don't reflect real life dynamics
  • When life changes, your plan doesn't adapt to fit those changes
  • Many tools doing a poor job, scattered across dozens of apps

Solia exists to bridge the gap between who you are now and who you want to become.
A system that aligns actions and outcomes, turning your dreams into reality.

01

Adaptive Scheduling

One schedule for your whole life — with reusable templates for the structures you repeat, so planning never starts from zero.

  • A schedule that intelligently aligns actions with desired outcomes
  • Templates that fit real-life flexibility while maintaining reusable structure
  • When life shifts, the schedule adapts instead of falling apart
02

Custom Toolkits

Dedicated toolkits for every specific area of your life — from fitness, to nutrition, to finances — all built into one system.

  • Each toolkit handles specific operations for each area of your life
  • Purposefully designed and integrated naturally into your schedule
  • Many different productivity apps all live in one integrated place — Solia
03

Voice Control

Manage and change your schedule on the fly, all by voice — built to save you time and hassle.

  • Change plans as fast as you can say them
  • No complicated layouts, no excessive work, no friction
  • Intelligent AI-driven language processing
04

Intelligent Coaching

A coaching layer built on your data — it learns from what you actually do and keeps your actions aligned with your goals.

  • Guidance toward your specific goals, not just generic tips
  • Built on what you actually do, not just what you planned to do
  • Learns from your patterns and adjusts to guide you towards your goals
05

Real Accountability

Constant check-ins and daily reporting hold you to the standard you set.

  • Daily reports that tell the truth about how your day went vs. how it was planned
  • Consistent check-ins that make sure your plans never quietly slip away
  • Every single day, a clear honest path from who you are now to who you are becoming
06

Competition Motivation

A ranked system that turns self-improvement into a game worth winning.

  • A ranked ladder for self-improvement and successful life metrics
  • Ranks earned from real progress, consistency, and results
  • Competition that pushes you to be better and gives you honest feedback on your life
07

Social Media

A new way to connect and share your life in real time — with the people chasing their dreams alongside you.

  • Share the life you're actually living, in real time
  • A feed full of people building and self improving, not performing for attention
  • Build connections with people of similar goals and mindsets, not surface level validation
