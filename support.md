# Support

The fastest way to reach us is email. A founder reads every message.

**[support@getsolia.com](mailto:support@getsolia.com)**

## When you write, include

- The email address on your Solia account
- Your device model and iOS version
- What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or screen recording if you have one — it usually cuts the back-and-forth in half

We aim to reply within **2 business days**.

## Bug reports from inside the app

The in-app **Help** section (Settings → Help) files bugs and suggestions directly to us, with the technical context attached automatically — it is the best route for anything you can reproduce.

## Account and data

- **Delete your account:** in the app under Settings → Account Management → Delete account, or see [the deletion page](/delete-account)
- **Privacy and data requests:** [privacy@getsolia.com](mailto:privacy@getsolia.com) — see the [Privacy Policy](/terms#privacy-policy)
- **Legal:** [legal@getsolia.com](mailto:legal@getsolia.com) — see the [Terms & Agreements](/terms)
