# Solia

Solia is a Life Operating System designed to align your actions with your goals in order to achieve desired outcomes.

Plan your day in schedule blocks, live it with real check-ins, track your fitness and nutrition, and get graded daily by an AI coach — with a global leaderboard where real days compete.
